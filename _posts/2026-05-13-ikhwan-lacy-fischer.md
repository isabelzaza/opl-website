Congratulations to Ikhwan Jeon for receiving a Lacy-Fischer grant for his project, *Mirroring Biological Optimization in Deep Neural Networks for Human-Aligned Visual Object Recognition*. The project is co-investigated by Isabel Gauthier (Psychology, Radiology and Radiological Sciences), Thomas Palmeri (Psychology, Ophthalmology and Visual Sciences), and Daniel Moyer (Computer Science).

The Lacy-Fischer grant supports students through and beyond their academic careers at Vanderbilt, creating unique research and professional development opportunities that broaden students' perspectives and enhance their work. These opportunities allow researchers to immerse themselves in the communities and scientific questions that their research seeks to serve.
